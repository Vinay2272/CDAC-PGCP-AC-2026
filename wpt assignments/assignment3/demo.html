Essay

Holi is a vibrant and joyful festival celebrated in India, marking the arrival of spring. Known as the “festival of colors,” it brings people together to play with colored powders, splash water, and share sweets. The celebration begins with Holika Dahan, symbolizing the victory of good over evil. On the next day, friends and families gather to enjoy music, dance, and festive foods like gujiya and thandai. Holi promotes unity, love, and forgiveness, as people forget past differences and celebrate together. It is a time of happiness, togetherness, and spreading positivity through colors and smiles.
